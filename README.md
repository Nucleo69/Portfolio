# Sara Patil / Portfolio

A minimalist editorial portfolio website for **Sara Patil** — Undergraduate Biomedical Science Scholar at NMIMS University (Mumbai), former Invited Visiting Student at the University of Zürich (UZH) Vetsuisse Faculty, and classical artist.

Designed with an editorial monograph aesthetic: warm ivory canvas, deep forest green accents, charcoal typography, and refined Swiss typography.

---

## Visual & Editorial Direction

- **Palette**: Warm ivory (`#fcfbf7`), deep forest green (`#1e3a2b`), charcoal typography (`#18191a`), with subtle muted burgundy (`#782d3b`).
- **Typography**: Large editorial serif (`Cormorant Garamond`) paired with contemporary sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) and monospace indices (`JetBrains Mono`).
- **Tone**: Understated, intelligent, curious, and personal. Free of emojis, em dashes, and generic neon gradients.
- **Languages**: 5-language translation engine (English, Deutsch, Marathi, Hindi, Korean) selectable via minimalist text controls.
- **Portraits**: Dual-mode portrait display (Monochrome and Natural Color).
- **Galleries**: Understated hover reveals for personal studies and fine art (Attack on Titan canvas, Dark Souls ink illustration, Demon Slayer character studies, classical dance, and cultural photography).
- **Zürich Feature**: Editorial spotlight on research at UZH Vetsuisse as an Invited Visiting Student investigating SARS-CoV-2 neuro-invasiveness, paired with architectural and landscape photography across Switzerland.

## Zero-Terminal Hosting (GitHub in Browser)

1. Open **[github.com](https://github.com)** in Safari or Chrome and sign in.
2. Click **New** repository and name it (e.g. `portfolio` or `<username>.github.io`). Make sure **Public** is selected.
3. On the repository page, click the link that says **"uploading an existing file"**.
4. In Finder on your Mac (`Desktop > Sara`), select:
   - `index.html`
   - `assets` (folder)
   - `README.md`
5. Drag and drop them into the GitHub browser window, then click **Commit changes**.
6. In your GitHub repository, click **Settings** ➔ **Pages** (on the left menu).
7. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/(root)`, then click **Save**.
8. In about 60–90 seconds, your website will be live.
